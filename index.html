Rules

Non-Negotiables

🌾
Isabgol: Water is Non-Negotiable
Always in 300ml+ warm water. Stir fast, drink immediately, then a second glass. Never dry. 2 hour gap from all other medications — it blocks drug absorption.
🦠
Probiotics: Heat Kills Them
Keep in the fridge. Take 30 min before lunch. No hot chai, coffee, or warm milk within 30 min — above 40°C destroys the live cultures.
🔬
Enzymes: Before, Not After
The Healthy Hey Multi-Enzyme must be taken just before or at the very start of eating. After the meal, they do nothing. 3× daily — every main meal.
💊
Becosules Z: Never Empty Stomach
B-vitamins cause nausea and vomiting on an empty stomach. Always after breakfast, not first thing in the morning.
😴
Melatonin: Cycle It
Max 3–5 nights, then a break. Daily use suppresses your body's natural melatonin. The Magnesium + Ashwagandha combo is your permanent sleep stack.
🔮
Magnesium: Start with 1 Capsule
The Citrate form can cause loose stools if you start at 2 capsules. 1 capsule weeks 1–2, increase to 2 from week 3 once your gut has adapted.
🧪
Blood Test at 3 Months
Get: B12, Vitamin D 25-OH, Zinc, Iron/Ferritin, TSH. Tell your doctor you take Biotin — it affects thyroid test results. Stop Biotin 72h before the test.
💧
2.5–3 Litres of Water Daily
Most supplement absorption is water-dependent. Headaches, constipation, and brain fog are often just dehydration. Start every morning with a glass of water.
