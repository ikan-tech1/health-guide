Daily Protocol

When to Take What

Follow this schedule every day for maximum absorption. Timing matters — fat-soluble vitamins need food, probiotics need to be away from meals.

🌅 Morning 7–9am
💊 B12 Melts 1 strip — place on tongue, let dissolve. Empty stomach OK. Sublingual = fastest absorption.
☀️ D3+K2 Melts 1 strip — with or just after breakfast. D3 is fat-soluble; a little food helps.
💅 Biotin Melts 1 strip — on tongue, morning. Can take same time as B12 and D3 strips.
🍳 Breakfast With meal
⚡ Becosules Z 1 capsule AFTER food — not on empty stomach (causes nausea). B-complex + zinc + vitamin C.
🧠 Brahmi 1 capsule with food — start with 1 daily; after 2 weeks increase to 2/day.
🌿 Digestive Enzymes 1–2 capsules just before or at start of meal. Take with every main meal.
☀️ Lunch 12–2pm
🦠 Probiotics 1 capsule, 30 min before lunch (on semi-empty stomach). Away from hot drinks. Most critical — consistency matters most here.
🐟 Omega-3 1–2 capsules with your largest meal of the day. Fat-soluble — always take with food containing some fat.
🌿 Digestive Enzymes 1 capsule at start of lunch. Helps break down food; reduces bloating after meals.
🌆 Dinner 7–9pm
🌿 Ashwagandha 1–2 capsules with dinner — evening dose for sleep benefits. The KSM-66 form is best absorbed with meals.
🌿 Digestive Enzymes 1 capsule with dinner — especially important for your main cooked meal.
🍵 Post Dinner 30–60 min after
🌾 Isabgol 1–2 tsp in 300ml+ warm water — stir well, drink immediately, then follow with another glass of water. Never take dry. Never take less than a full glass of water.
🌿 Triphala 1–2 tablets — taken 30–60 min after dinner. Acts as a gentle overnight gut tonic. Allow at least 2h after Isabgol.
🌙 Bedtime 30 min before sleep
🔮 Triple Magnesium Week 1: 1 capsule. Week 2+: 2 capsules. Start low, ramp up. Take with warm water. Magnesium Glycinate helps you sleep, Threonate supports the brain overnight.
💤 Melatonin 1 capsule (10mg), only when needed — not every single night. Use for 3–5 nights to reset your cycle, then take breaks. 10mg is high — consider splitting tablet.
🤕 As Needed Not daily
🔴 Saridon 1–2 tablets with food for headache. Never on empty stomach. Max 3 tablets/day, max 3 days in a row. Not a daily supplement.

Safety

Combinations & Timing Rules

What can be taken together and what needs to be spaced apart.

ℹ️
General rule: Fat-soluble vitamins (D3, K2, Omega-3, Biotin) always need food. Water-soluble (B12, C, Becosules) can be taken any time. Isabgol and fibre supplements need 2 hours away from all medications.
B12 Melts + D3 Melts + Biotin All three in the morning — fine together ✓
Probiotics + Hot Drinks (chai/coffee) Heat kills beneficial bacteria — wait 30 min ✗
Isabgol + Any Medication Space 2 hours apart — husk reduces drug absorption !
Omega-3 + Vitamin D3 Omega-3 actually enhances D3 absorption — great combo ✓
Magnesium + Isabgol Both relax the gut — together can cause loose stools. Space by 3+ hours. !
Ashwagandha + Melatonin Strong sedative synergy at night — start with one before combining !
Becosules Z + Empty Stomach B-vitamins cause nausea on empty stomach — always after food ✗
Digestive Enzymes + Probiotics Take 30 min apart — enzymes can break down probiotic capsule shells !
Tea/Coffee + B12 Absorption Tannins in tea reduce B12 absorption — take B12 30 min before tea !
Saridon + Alcohol Paracetamol + alcohol = liver stress. Strictly avoid. ✗

Pro Tips

Make the Stack Work Harder

Supplements amplify a good lifestyle — they don't replace it. These compound the effect of everything you ordered.

💧
Hydrate First, Supplement Second
Drink 2.5–3 litres of water daily. Most supplement absorption is water-dependent. Headaches, constipation, and brain fog are often just dehydration in disguise.
🥣
Food Quality Matters
Supplements patch holes, they don't build the foundation. Add curd/yoghurt daily (probiotic + calcium), ghee with meals (fat for D3 absorption), and a banana in the morning (prebiotic + magnesium).
🌅
Morning Sunlight — Free D3
10–15 minutes of sunlight on skin before 10am generates natural Vitamin D3. This compounds with your D3 supplement dramatically. Step outside every morning.
📵
Screen Cut-Off for Melatonin
Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin production by up to 3 hours. Enable Night Mode / True Tone or wear blue-light glasses after 8pm. This makes your Melatonin and Magnesium 2x more effective.
🧘
Consistency Over Dosage
Missing one day won't set you back — missing a week will. Set a morning and evening phone alarm for your two main supplement windows. The effect is cumulative and compounds over months.
🔬
Get Bloodwork After 3 Months
Test: Vitamin B12, Vitamin D25-OH, Zinc, Iron panel (ferritin), and Thyroid (TSH). This tells you which supplements are working, what to increase, and whether you need anything else like Iron or Iodine.
🚫
What to Cut During the Gut Reset
For the first 4 weeks: minimise refined sugar, excessive tea/coffee, and ultra-processed food. These feed bad gut bacteria and undo what your Probiotics + Triphala are building.
💊
Store Probiotics Correctly
Keep your Wellbeing Probiotics in the refrigerator. Heat above 40°C can kill the live cultures. Take the bottle out for 2 minutes before use — don't store them in a hot car or shelf.
